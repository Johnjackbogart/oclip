openclip

A free, open-source clipper plugin in the spirit of GClip. Three controls, nothing else.

Free and open source (AGPL-3.0). Older versions on the releases page; source on GitHub.

Controls

Gain

Input drive in dB, applied before the clipper.

Clip Amount

Threshold/ceiling in dB. Lower = clips harder.

Softness

Blends a hard brick-wall clip into smooth tanh saturation.

Install

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy openclip.vst3 (and/or openclip.clap) into your plugin folder: macOS ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/ · Windows C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ · Linux ~/.vst3/ and ~/.clap/
  3. Rescan plugins in your DAW.
macOS: the plugin isn’t notarized, so Gatekeeper will quarantine the download. After copying it into place, clear the flag and rescan: 
xattr -rd com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/openclip.vst3

Built in Rust on nice-plug. AGPL-3.0 licensed. AU (Logic/GarageBand) and oversampling are not implemented yet.
